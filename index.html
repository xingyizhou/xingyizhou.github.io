I am a Scientist at AMI Labs. My current research focuses on building long-form video models.

I received my Ph.D. at The University of Texas at Austin, supervised by Prof. Philipp Krähenbühl. Before that, I obtained my bachelor degree from School of Computer Science at Fudan University.

I have worked at Meta SuperIntelligence Labs and Google DeepMind, and interned at Microsoft Research Asia, Google Research, Intel Labs, and Facebook AI Research. My work has contributed to Meta's video generation models, and Gemini 2.5.
